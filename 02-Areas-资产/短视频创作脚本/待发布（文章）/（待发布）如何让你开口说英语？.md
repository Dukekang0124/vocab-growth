How can you start speaking English?

The most important thing about learning a language is using it,

not just reading about it in a book.

First, think about where you want to use it.

Let me give you an example.

I am learning English by myself and I have two goals:

The first one is to talk smoothly with people from other countries and make friends.

I have already started this, and I can talk a little with people from other countries.

The second one is to read books in English.

For reading English books,

I am starting with children's English books

because we need to start from the basics.

Some grown-ups don't want to read children's books,

but if we try to read books with big words, it's hard to keep going.

I think if you do these five things every day,

your English will get much better:

The first is to listen more:

  Listen to English songs and watch English cartoons,

  just like listening to stories,

  so your ears get used to the sound of English.

The second is to speak more:

Don't be afraid of making mistakes.

Talk in English like you talk with your friends,

even if you don't speak well at first.

The third is to imitate more:

Find an English-speaking character you like and copy how they talk,

so your pronunciation will get closer to theirs.

The fourth is to practice more:

You can practice in front of a mirror at home or with friends who want to learn English.

They can help you hear where you might be saying something wrong.

The fifth is to ask more:

If someone says your pronunciation is not right,

don't be sad. Ask them how to say it,

so you can learn more.

Learning anything takes time

and the right way,

then you can do it too.
