I think it's very important to stop doubting yourself!

Because when you doubt yourself, you're actually stopping yourself from getting better.

You focus too much on all the bad things in life

and all the areas where you haven't made progress,

instead of focusing on all the good things you've done

and all the areas where you are getting better.

  

So if you stop paying attention to bad things

and focus more on the good things,

You'll see the success you've had

and the progress you've made.

This will only make you more confident in yourself

and believe you can achieve the goals you set.

  

If you do this every day—

focus on the good things and improving yourself—

You will make progress and see the success you want!
