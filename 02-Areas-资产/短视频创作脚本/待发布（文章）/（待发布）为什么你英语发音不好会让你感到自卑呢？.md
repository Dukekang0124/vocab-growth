Why does bad English speaking make you feel bad about yourself?

  

Think about when you were little.

If you were in a class where everyone spoke English,

but your English was not quite right,

Other kids might not understand you or think it's funny.

You might feel shy and worried that people will laugh at you.

That's what feeling bad about yourself feels like.

  

How can you not feel bad about yourself?

  

It's like learning to ride a bike.

You might fall at first,

but if you practice a lot,

you get better.

For English speaking,

We need to practice a lot and not be afraid of making mistakes.

Each time you talk, think of it as a chance to practice.

  

What should you do exactly?

  

1. Listen more:
    
    1. Like listening to stories,
        
    2. listening to English songs and watching English cartoons.
        
    3. This way, your ears get used to the sound of English.
        
2. Speak more:
    
    1. Don't be afraid of making mistakes.
        
    2. Talk in English like you chat with friends
        
    3. even if it's not good at first.
        
3. Imitate more:
    
    1. Find an English character you like and copy how they talk.
        
    2. Your pronunciation will get closer to theirs.
        
4. Practice more:
    
    1. Practice in front of a mirror at home
        
    2. or with friends who want to learn English.
        
    3. Let them listen and tell you what's not good.
        
5. Ask more:
    
    1. If someone says your pronunciation is wrong,
        
    2. don't be sad.
        
    3. Ask them how to say it right,
        
    4. so you can learn more.
        

  

So, everyone starts from not knowing to knowing,

just like learning to walk.

You might fall at first,

but after walking a lot,

You can run very fast.

Learning English is the same.

Don't feel bad because you can't speak well at first.

Practice more, and you will get better and better!
