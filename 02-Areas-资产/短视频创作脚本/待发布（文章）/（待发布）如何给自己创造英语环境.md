First, you need to get used to having English around you all the time.

For example, change your phone language to English.

because everything on your phone will be in English and it feels **strange** at first.

But you have to overcome this.

After a while, you'll get used to it.

Second, use your ears.

When you're walking, driving, or doing **chores**,

you can listen to English.

For example, there are English **podcasts.**

I **suggest** you use an app called "Daily English Listening".

It has **popular** English podcasts like "**Studio** Classroom" and "Learning Easy English".

There are many **programs** to **choose** from.

The most important thing is to get used to the speed of English conversations and how people use English.

You might not understand a lot

because you don't know many words or **grammar rules** yet.

But that's okay. Just listen to what you can understand now.

Third, use what you see, hear, learn, and remember.
