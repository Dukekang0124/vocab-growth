I think the first thing is to change our thinking a little bit.

  

Focus on what being alone brings you, and what this stage of life brings you.

  

Whether people stay with you for a long time or pass by briefly.

  

So if you don’t have a good relationship with yourself,

  

Our relationships or friendships with others can’t solve this problem either.

  

The more we understand and love ourselves,

  

The more we can help others in the future.

  

I guess the next question should be:

  

How can I become an independent person?

  

First, how can I enjoy being alone?

  

Don’t fight against it.

  

You are returning to yourself, to your true self.

  

This is an exciting moment! You will figure out who you are and what you want.

  

Do the things you like when no one is influencing you or pushing you in any direction.
