In this time, we have lots of knowledge,

but what we really need is our attention.

No matter what you want to learn,

you can find it on the internet.

No matter what you want to know,

you can find ways on the internet.

Do you remember the goals you set in 2024?

Did you achieve them?

Why didn't you achieve last year's goals?

Because you paid more attention to other things that are not important.

  

Where you put your attention,

that's where your success will be.

You need to understand that attention is your most precious treasure.

It's a choice.

We should spend our valuable time

and attention on the most important things.

We need to cut out the less important things,

or we will be stuck in a meaningless place.

For us ordinary people,

we can make a long list of things like work, family, friends, and learning.

  

We do one and add another,

but we can never finish them all,

and we waste our precious attention.

Because doing those unimportant things only makes us feel better at the time,

but they don't really help us reach our goals.

So, to save our attention,

we should spend less time deciding on unimportant things

and put our attention on the important things,

which will have long-term value.
