Or is it because you just don't want to learn English?

  

Do you think learning English is a very painful thing?

Yes, learning can be a painful thing.

When we face other fun things,

like watching funny videos, dance videos, gossip videos, and so on,

compared to learning English,

it's a boring thing that makes it hard for you to keep going.

To be honest,

I once gave up learning English

because I always forgot words, couldn't learn phonetics, didn't understand grammar, and it was even harder to speak English.

  

At that time,

I really thought it was so hard,

and I felt like I wasn't the kind of person who could learn English.

I felt like a person who even I looked down on.

But when I saw videos of older people learning English,

saw those who were still posting their self-study English practice every day,

saw that they were still persisting,

saw how confident those who had learned English were,

and looked at myself, I felt so embarrassed,

  

I told myself I couldn't give up,

because I need to tell the world

that if we can't stick to our own choices,

what else can we do?

All the difficulties in front of us are self-imposed.

We really want something,

but we dare not express it.

We know it's right to do this thing,

but we make all kinds of excuses.

Let ourselves always be in regret.

Because time waits for no one,

what you need to do

and the only thing you can do is stick to your choice.
