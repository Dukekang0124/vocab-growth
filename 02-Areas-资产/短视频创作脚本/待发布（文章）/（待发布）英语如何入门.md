I think the best way to start learning English is to read a lot,

just like foreign little kids do.

Because it can help you learn more words and get a feel for English.

Most importantly,

it can help you keep going.

If you can finish a children's English book,

your focus will get better,

and you will feel more confident in learning English.

  

For example,

most of the content in children's English books is something we can understand.

A lot of times we can't keep going

because the things we learn are not right for our level.

It's mostly because we don't know enough words

and the grammar is too hard,

so we can't understand the materials we are learning.

When we can read children's English books,

you will find there are many simple words in them.

They can help you remember the words you have learned before

and also learn some new words.

  

In this process,

we can keep building our vocabulary.

The most important thing is that many children's English books are stories,

which are fun and easy to understand with basic grammar.

Whether you are learning English by yourself or teaching your kids,

it is one of the best ways.
