If you want a better life to get fame, money, or cool things like houses and cars (not to grow up and be a better you), the happy feeling will go away when you get them. Then you might ask: "What's next?"

  

I think that's why many people get stuck in a "never-happy" trap. They focus on stuff outside them, but forget that making yourself better is the real key to lasting happiness. When your goal isn't about being better, even if you reach it, you'll feel empty – like holding sand. The tighter you squeeze, the faster it falls out!**
