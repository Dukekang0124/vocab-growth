Today, I want to share how I learn English by myself and the tools I use.

I wake up early every day and use "Duolingo" to do exercises.

Then, I practice listening with "Daily English Listening"

and read English articles out loud.

I really care about speaking,

so I use the app "HelloTalk" to talk to foreigners in English.

This is very important. English is a tool for speaking,

and we need to use it in practice.

Of course, many people worry about not speaking well. But think about it this way, if you don't speak well, can't you still make mistakes?

The only person who will really point out your mistakes is yourself.

Don't care too much about what others think. Learning is always a process. English is a skill worth having for a long time because a lot of important information is in English.

We need English to communicate and read to learn new things and get new chances.
