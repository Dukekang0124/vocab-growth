## 这本书解决什么问题

> "People will probably understand you if you say 'making your homework'... but your language will sound unnatural."

**搭配（collocation）** = 词的自然组合。母语者说 `do homework` / `make a mistake`，不说 `make homework` / `do a mistake`。**这就是「语法对但听着怪」和「地道」的区别**——直接命中你「输出给老外」的目标。

## 核心高频搭配速查（聊天直接能用）

### 常见错误 vs 地道搭配
| ❌ 不地道 | ✅ 地道 |
|:--|:--|
| make homework | **do** homework |
| do a mistake | **make** a mistake |
| get ill (勉强) | **fall** ill |
| very happy (泛) | **over the moon** / **thrilled** |
| big rain (勉强) | **heavy** rain |
| strong rain (错) | **heavy** rain |
| do a choice (错) | **make** a choice |
| say a lie (错) | **tell** a lie |
