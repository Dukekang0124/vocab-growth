## 学习价值（为什么对口）

1. **场景 = 聊天话题**：想练「酒店入住」→ 找 Daily Life - Hotel Upgrade；想练「职场请求」→ The Office 课程
2. **对话是完整的**：不是孤立句子，是 A/B 一来一回——正好模拟你跟老外聊天
3. **词汇带释义**：每课词汇表直接可用
4. **分级明确**：Elementary（初级）→ Intermediate → Advanced

## 经典课示例

**C0004 The Office - I need an assistant!**（职场请求）
> A: ...like I told you before, we just don't have the resources to hire you an assistant.
> B: I understand that, but the fact is we're understaffed.
> A: The timing is just not right. The economy is bad, and it's too risky to take on new staff.

**可学表达**：we don't have the resources / the fact is / we're understaffed / the timing is not right / it's too risky to

**C0005 Daily Life - Cut In Line**（排队/旅行）
> A: I can't believe it took us two hours to get here. The traffic in New York is unbelievable.
> B: Yeah, but just relax honey, we're here and we're going on vacation.

**可学表达**：I can't believe it took us / the traffic is unbelievable / just relax
