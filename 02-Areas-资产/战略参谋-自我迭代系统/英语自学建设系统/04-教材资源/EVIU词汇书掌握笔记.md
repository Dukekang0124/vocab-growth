## ⭐ 高频错误库（38条精选，聊天前必看）

### 最容易犯的 10 条（按场景）

| 错误说法 ❌ | 正确说法 ✅ | 场景 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| He's english. | He's **English**. | 国家名大写 |
| She's got straight hairs. | She's got straight **hair**. | hair 不可数 |
| How is he like? | **What's** he like? | 问性格 |
| I like your new trouser. | I like your new **trousers**. | trousers 复数 |
| He's a good cooker. | He's a good **cook**. | cook=人，cooker=厨具 |
| I was very stressing. | I was very **stressed**. | -ed 形容人 |
| It was a good travel. | It was a good **journey**. | travel 不可数 |
| He has 30. | He's **30 years old**. | 年龄用be |
| I take the steak. | I'll **have** the steak. | 点餐用have |
| I like being in the nature. | I like being in **nature** / surrounded by **nature**. | 不要the |

### 其他高频（按主题）

**打电话**：Is that Joe? (NOT Are you Joe?) / It's Lily. (NOT I am Lily)
**工作**：I have a lot of work. (NOT works) / She gives me advice. (NOT advise) / I had some training. (NOT a training) / He didn't have a job. (NOT a work)
**音乐**：Classical music (NOT classic music)
**旅行**：My flight number is BA640. (NOT my fly number)
**婚姻**：She's getting married **to** Darren. (NOT with)
**年龄**：a 30-**year**-old man (NOT years)
**新闻**：It says in the paper / According to the paper (NOT It's written in the paper)
