## 第四批：traditional + western（2词，文化对比主题）

| 词 | 等级 | 简单英语解释 | 例句（能直接用） |
|:--|:--:|:--|:--|
| **traditional** | 🟢 高频 | something old, done the same way for a long time, like a family's old custom | I like trying traditional food when I travel. |
| **western** | 🟢 高频 | from the west side of the world (Europe/America), or from the west part of a country | Western people usually don't eat dog meat, but we don't either! |

**串句练习**（文化对比，直接可聊）：
> *I like both traditional Chinese food and western food. The best thing is when western people try Chinese hot pot for the first time—they always say it's amazing!*

**学习要点**：
- traditional 反义词 modern；词族 tradition→traditional→traditionally
- western 词族 west→western；「老外」最自然说法=Westerners
- 文化对比（中vs西）是聊天安全话题

## 第五批：stuff + productive（2词，口语高频）

| 词 | 等级 | 简单英语解释 | 例句（能直接用） |
|:--|:--:|:--|:--|
| **stuff** | 🟢 口语超高频 | things—any things at all, when you don't want to name them exactly | I'm busy with job stuff these days. ⚠️不可数无stuffs |
| **productive** | 🟢 高频 | doing a lot of useful work in a short time, finishing things | I had a really productive day today—I learned 18 new words! |

**串句练习**（直接可聊）：
> *I had a really productive day today. I learned a bunch of new English stuff, practiced with my language partner, and even recorded a video. I love days like this!*

**学习要点**：
- stuff 是 things 的口语版，万能填充词；⚠️不可数（no stuffs）
- productive 词族：produce→product→productive→productivity；反义 unproductive
- 常用搭配：a productive day / a productive meeting

## 第六批：newspaper + editor（2词，媒体主题）

| 词 | 等级 | Simple English | 语块 | 例句（能直接用） |
|:--|:--:|:--|:--|:--|
| newspaper | 🟢 高频 | a big paper with news about what is happening in the world | read the newspaper / in the newspaper | I want to start reading an English newspaper to practice. |
| editor | 🟡 阅读高频 | a person who checks and fixes writing or videos before people see them | work as an editor / the editor of... | I'm the editor of my own channel. |

**串句练习**：
> *I'm the editor of my own channel. I make videos, edit them, and sometimes read English newspapers to find interesting stories to share.*

**学习要点**：
- newspaper = news + paper；「看报纸」= read the newspaper（不是look）
- editor 词族：edit→editor→editorial；video editor=视频剪辑师
- editor 完美贴合自媒体身份
