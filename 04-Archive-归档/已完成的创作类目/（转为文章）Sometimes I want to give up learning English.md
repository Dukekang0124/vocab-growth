Sometimes I want to stop learning English every day.

It's really tiring to keep going.

But then I think: 
If I can't even stick to learning English,
what else can I do well?

And more than that,
I want to use English to see the world, 
and make friends from all over the world.

To learn English well, you have to show up every day.

Listen a little, read a little, speak a little, every single day.

Little by little, your English will get better.

Maybe in one year, maybe in three years.

If you push through the hard days, 

you'll learn so much more than you ever thought possible.

We are all ordinary people, but we have time.

Investing time in learning English is the best investment you can make.

So many people give up halfway.

Only the strongest people keep going, and beat their own laziness.

They show up and try their best, on all those quiet, unremarkable days.

And in the end, you'll realize how amazing you really are.
