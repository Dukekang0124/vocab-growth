Stay focused, fully focused, totally focused on your own work.

No one cares about you, nor your useless anxiety.

Gather your attention and put it on what matters most.

All your worries and anxiety come from chasing vague, distant things,

instead of doing the clear, real things right at hand.

If you want to improve your English,

here are 3 pieces of advice for you.

First, you have to have real love for English.

If you have no passion for it, you’ll never stick with it.

There will be many days when you get no feedback from learning.

It truly tests your willpower, and whether you can get through hard times.

Learning a language is always a long journey.

Second, don’t be afraid to make mistakes.

It’s totally okay if you pronounce words wrong.

Most of the time, native speakers don’t care at all.

Besides, you don’t have that many people watching you.

Everyone is busy with their own lives.

What you need to do is beat your own laziness.

Third, grow from your failures.

Hard work doesn’t always pay off right away.

Lots of times, you try for a long time and still see no progress.

But if you believe you can make it, you can keep going for a long time.

As for all those learning methods, there are so many of them.

You don’t need to worry about that.

Just try any method that you think you can stick to.

That’s how you find your own motivation and take action.
For each of us, 
the most important thing is not to look at the vague and distant things, 
but to do the clear and real things at hand.
