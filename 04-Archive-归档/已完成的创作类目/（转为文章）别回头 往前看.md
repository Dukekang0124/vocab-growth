If you focus on what you've left behind, 
you'll never be able to see what lies ahead."
 
It means:
If you only think about the past, 
you will never see the beautiful future in front of you.
 
Life is like this.
We all have bad days,
small mistakes, 
or things we feel sad about.
But we can’t keep looking back.
 
We should look forward, 
try our best, and move on.
 
Every new day is a new chance.
Just keep going.
