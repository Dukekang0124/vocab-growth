Stop making excuses for yourself.  
If you want to get something done,
you have to start doing it—  
not just sit there daydreaming without taking action.

You know what? I failed lots of times.  

For a long time, I was really shy. I didn’t dare to say what I thought.  
People looked at me and thought I was nobody.  

I grew up in a place where people always told me I was wrong.  
I even thought about killing myself.  
I hated them. They made me feel so bad inside.  
I heard so many mean words.  

Back then, I felt like I had no soul.  
It took me a long, long time to feel better inside.  

Now they are nice to me. I don’t want to think about the sad stuff anymore.  

But for a while, I kept making excuses.  
I wanted to do something good — but I just sat there and dreamed.  
I didn’t do it.  
Why? Because I was scared I’d fail. I wanted everything to be perfect.  

But now I know — I can’t live like this anymore.  
I have to walk out of the dark all by myself.  
I need to hold that little scared kid inside me and say, “It’s okay.”  
I need to change how I think. I need to believe in me.  

’Cause if I don’t even believe in myself...  
Who will?  

If I don’t even try to think about what I really want,  
and how to get it...  
Then what can I ever really do?  

So I want to tell you — we have to save ourselves.  
We have to do the things we think are right.  
Even if it’s hard.  

All the good things come from what we do NOW.  
Not from what we just dream about.  

I know — when we try new things, we feel scared. We stop.  
We’re afraid it won’t work.  
But you know what? Life is here for us to feel.  
If we don’t even try — how will we know how great it feels?  

The answer is: just start.  
Start even if you’re not sure.  
Keep going, and the fear will go away.  
You’ll believe in yourself more and more.  

If I can do it... why can’t you?
