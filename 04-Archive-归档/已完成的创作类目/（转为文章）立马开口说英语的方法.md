Hey my friends!

Today, I want to share a way to start speaking English right away!

First, think:

What words do you know?

What English sentences have you learned?

Once you know these,

try to make sentences with the words you know.

Don’t worry about grammar—

just follow your feelings!

Then,

open the **HelloTalk** app

and say your sentences to someone from another country.

If you feel shy or scared,

here’s another idea:

Use an AI app!

Speak English to the AI.

To speak English, you need to be brave!

Here’s a tip:

**Start where you are**.

Keep practicing and fixing mistakes—

you’ll improve fast! 💪
