It’s Okay to Do Nothing Sometimes 
Our busy world makes it feel strange to just relax. 
We are often told to work, to keep busy, 
to do something all the time. 
Is this really good for us? 
Why does society make us think resting is bad?
You will find that doing nothing is not only nice, 
but also very important for our health and a balanced life. 
You work hard or keep busy with side jobs all the time. 
You know resting is important. 
Your body and mind need rest to get back energy.
But if you stop working or doing things, 
you feel uneasy inside. 
You always have too many things to finish. 
But if you don’t rest, 
you will feel even worse. 
Your body and mind will get more and more tired. 
Rest helps your brain relax. 
Sometimes, doing nothing is the best thing. 
We always feel so much pressure to stay busy and productive. 
It seems society expects us to work or find things to do nonstop.
If we aren’t busy,
people may think we are lazy or not good enough.
Everyone around us is busy, 
so we feel we have to be busy too. 
Our culture values being busy more than taking care of ourselves. 
But rest is not a waste of time. 
It is very important for our health and happiness. 
If we don’t rest, 
our bodies and brains get very tired and stressed. 
We can’t focus on the moment or do our best. 
So we need real rest.
No TV, no phones, no distractions. 
Just focus on your breathing. 
This helps your body and mind relax.
When you let yourself do nothing, 
it’s important to stay in the moment. 
Don’t worry about future things or think about the past. 
Just focus on right now. 
When you live in the moment and don’t worry about other times, 
you can truly relax.
